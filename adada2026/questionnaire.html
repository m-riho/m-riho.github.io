Instrument guide / Q1–Q18

How to read this questionnaire

These are the published English wordings from the related law-student studies. The ADADA 2026 poster reports a separate, cross-disciplinary sample and analyzes selected items. This guide does not imply that every item was administered to that sample.

Q1–Q3, Q5–Q6 and Q15–Q17 follow the Journal for Geometry and Graphics (JGG). Q4, including its five response options, follows Appendix A.1.2 of the Journal of Information Processing (JIP) manuscript. Q7–Q14 and Q18 also use the full questions from the JIP appendix. The JIP authors translated the original Japanese questions into English.

Five-point agreement scale

  1. 1 = Strongly disagree
  2. 2 = Disagree
  3. 3 = Neutral
  4. 4 = Agree
  5. 5 = Strongly agree

JGG specifies the two endpoints and the neutral midpoint; “Disagree” and “Agree” are explanatory labels for the intermediate values. Q4, Q6 and Q18 do not use this scale.

Section B / Q4–Q6

Prompt Writing

Q4. What was the most difficult aspect when writing prompts?

Multiple answers allowed · JIP Appendix A.1.2

  • Specification of coordinates and size (abstraction)
  • Arrangement and rotation of shapes (pattern recognition)
  • Design of procedures and repetition (algorithmic thinking)
  • Adjustment of color and atmosphere
  • Other (free description)

Five-point agreement scale · JGG §5.2

Open-ended response · JGG §5.4.1
